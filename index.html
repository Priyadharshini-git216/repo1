Gv Prakash

Actor | Singer | Music director

G.V. Prakash Kumar, also known as GVP, is an Indian musician, actor, and film producer who works predominantly in Tamil cinema. He is a recipient of three National Film Awards, four Filmfare Awards, and three SIIMA Awards. He became famous through his breakthrough as a film music composer in the mid-2000s, later cementing his stardom by transitioning into a lead film actor. He initially entered the industry as a child playback singer (contributing vocals to tracks for his uncle, A.R. Rahman, in films like Gentleman and Bombay)
